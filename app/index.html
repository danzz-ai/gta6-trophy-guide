    Road to the Platinum

      Missable trophies

      Launch-era guides agree: only one trophy is possibly missable. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story, and nothing on the 100% checklist is permanently missable (PowerPyx, GTABase).

        How to stay safe

        1. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.
        2. In Chapter 1, do Lucia's balcony meditation at Jason's house the morning after the mission Down to Earth.
        3. Keep one save from before the final mission, just in case.
